## 3. Known Issues

Some issues found during validation remain unresolved in this release, summarized as follows:

| No. | Issue | Description | Impact |
|-----|-------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | [#21485](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend/issues/21485) | On A5 with vLLM-Ascend 0.32.0RC, the D-node throughput of the GLM-5.3-Flash pooling (PD disaggregation) service regresses by up to approximately 50% in certain multi-prefix scenarios compared with v0.30.0, with the same model, hardware, service configuration, and workload. The root cause has not yet been identified. | D-node throughput of the pooling service on A5 can drop by up to ~50% in specific multi-prefix cases; the regression was observed in specific cases and should not be assumed to apply to all workloads; investigation is ongoing. |
| 2 | [#21486](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend/issues/21486) | During GLM-5.3-Flash W8A8 stress testing on A3 (pooling PD disaggregation, DP8/TP2, expert parallel, MTP speculative decoding, RecoverSSM), an intermittent, low-probability D-node service hang occurred after about 30 hours 41 minutes of running: a worker exception (`RuntimeError: numel: integer multiplication overflow`) in RecoverSSM speculative-metadata staging was the first fatal exception, after which EngineCore exited and the service could no longer process requests normally, with device execution failures and communication waits in other parallel processes. | Potential intermittent D-node hang (low probability) under sustained long-running stress; the staging-bound hypothesis (count > batch_size) remains unverified and the root cause is not confirmed; a RecoverSSM metadata bounds-validation fix has been referenced but the issue has not been declared fixed. |
| 3 | [#21487](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend/issues/21487) | Known nightly issue carried into 0.32.0RC: in the Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct audio-feature processing path (`_process_audio_input` in `qwen2_audio.py`), CANN/OPP reports `Unsupported_Operator(EZ3003)` — no supported ops kernel/engine is found for the Conv2D operator; EngineCore initialization consequently fails and the Qwen2-Audio accuracy test does not pass. | Qwen2-Audio is a known limitation on 0.32.0RC (currently confirmed to affect Qwen2-Audio only; no fix is planned on the RC branch); a CANN ops-package upgrade (version 1101) is planned on main to address operator support. |
